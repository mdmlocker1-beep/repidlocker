FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Reiod Locker.

What is Reiod Locker? ▼

Reiod Locker is an EMI Mobile Security and Device Management Software designed for mobile retailers and distributors in India. It allows you to remotely lock, track, and manage devices sold on installment plans.

How does Reiod Locker work? ▼

You install the Customer App on the buyer's phone at the time of purchase, activate it via the Retailer App, and then manage all devices remotely through your dashboard. You can lock, track, or send EMI alerts anytime.

How do I set up Reiod Locker on a phone? ▼

Download the Customer APK from our website, install it on the customer's device during purchase, then activate it using your Retailer App. The whole process takes less than 5 minutes. Watch our installation video for step-by-step guidance.

Can the customer remove the Reiod Locker app? ▼

No. Reiod Locker is protected against uninstallation. It uses device admin permissions and flash protection to prevent removal. Even if the customer tries to factory reset the device, the system has safeguards in place.

How do I become a distributor? ▼

Fill out the application form in the Distributor section above. Our team will review your application and contact you within 24–48 hours to discuss onboarding. Distributor recruitment is open across all states in India.

Is Reiod Locker legal in India? ▼

Yes. Reiod Locker operates in compliance with Indian laws. The lock system is based on a signed agreement between the retailer and buyer at the time of purchase. Both parties are informed of the device management software before installation. See our Terms & Conditions for full details.
